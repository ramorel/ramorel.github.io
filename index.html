About

I'm a data scientist and measurement methodologist. I design rigorous measurement systems and causal inference infrastructure for behavioral data at scale — connecting latent constructs to observable outcomes through Bayesian multilevel modeling, experimental and quasi-experimental design, psychometric measurement, and survey methodology.

Currently at YouTube, where I own the measurement roadmap for Marketing and lead a team of 30+ data scientists and researchers. Previously at Google and Meta, where I built measurement programs spanning advertiser trust, product experimentation, and the psychological impact of content exposure on human reviewers in trust & safety contexts.
